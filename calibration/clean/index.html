How we bake

The dough is mixed the afternoon before and left to prove overnight in the cool room. The oven is lit at four, and the first loaves come out a little before seven.

Flour comes from a mill forty miles up the coast. We bake a small amount of each loaf so that little goes to waste; what is left at closing goes to the shelter on Quay Street.
